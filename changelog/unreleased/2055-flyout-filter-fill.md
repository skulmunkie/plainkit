The open flyout filter panel of pk-data-table and pk-list-page fills the list height with Clear filters pinned at the bottom and stacks its controls one per row at the panel width. New filterPanelReflow (Blazor FilterPanelReflow) makes the table narrow beside the open panel on wide viewports so the right-most columns and the pager stay usable; a phone keeps the overlay.
