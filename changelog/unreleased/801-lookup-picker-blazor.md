`PkLookupPicker<TItem>` wraps `pk-lookup-picker` with a typed `Load` (the same `PkListRequest` and `PkListResult` as `PkDataTable`), typed `Columns`, `@bind-Value` and, with `Multiple`, `@bind-Values`, both with `EditForm` support, plus `SelectedLabels` and a `Resolve` callback for stored keys.
