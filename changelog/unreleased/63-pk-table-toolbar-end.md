A `pk-table` toolbar item with the attribute `data-end` (and the items after it) sits at the end of the toolbar row, so a search box and an action button can take opposite ends. The `columns` documentation now says what `type: date` and `type: number` do: they decide sorting and alignment only, and the cell shows the value as given, with no locale or time zone formatting.
