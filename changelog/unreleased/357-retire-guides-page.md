The standalone Guides page is retired, with no redirect: the Guides live only in the site app, and the old address stops working. Old to new: `site/guides/index.html` becomes `app.html#/guides`, `site/guides/index.html#/<guide>` becomes `app.html#/guides/<guide>`, and `site/guides/index.html#/<guide>/<heading>` becomes `app.html#/guides/<guide>?anchor=<heading>`.
