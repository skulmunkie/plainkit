`pk-lookup-picker` is a select-style field for a long list: its popup holds a searchable, pageable `pk-data-table` fed by the same `load(query)` callback, and the popup is built on the first open. Picking a row sets `value` to its key (the form value) and shows its label (`labelKey`, `selectedLabels` or the `resolve(keys)` callback). With `multiple` the popup table gets a checkbox column that survives paging and search (no select-all across pages) and stays open, the chosen rows show as removable chips, `values` holds the keys, `max` limits them, `pk-values-change` reports them and the form submits one entry per key; labels of keys not on a loaded page come from `selectedLabels` or one batched `resolve(keys)` call. Set the popup table with the plain props `columns`, `page-size` and `search-label`, or with `config` (a plain prop wins once it differs from its default). The field shows a chevron that turns over while the popup is open, and the invalid state draws the error colour on the border.
