The new guide "Size comparison" publishes what the same page (a validated form, a sortable paged table and a dialog) costs on a cold load in Plainkit, Lit, Svelte, Vue, React, Angular, Shoelace and Blazor, with the exact versions. The benchmark that produces it lives in `bench/size-comparison/` (not in any package, not in CI) and repeats with one command.
