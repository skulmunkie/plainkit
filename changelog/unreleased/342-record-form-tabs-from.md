`pk-record-form` shows sections by tab with no per-page CSS: tag sections in the form or the sidebar with `tab="key"` (several keys separated by spaces) and set `tabs-from` (`phone`, the default, `tablet` or `always`). At or below that width only the chosen tab's sections show; above it every section shows in the two-column layout and the tab strip is hidden. Blazor: `TabsFrom`.
