Blazor gets the typed page actions: the generated `PkAction` record, `NewAction`, `ToolbarActions` and `ActionsCollapse` on `PkListPage`, `Actions`, `NewAction` and `ActionsCollapse` on `PkToolPage`, `PkSettingsPage` and `PkSectionsPage`, `FooterActions` on `PkWizardPage`, and `OnAction` (by key) on the list page. There is no per-item callback.
