A data-driven `pk-table` row can carry `tone` (`warning`, `positive`, `accent`, `critical`) and `indent` (1 or 2) fields: the row is tinted with a leading rule, indented under its parent, and a phone card keeps the same rule. In Blazor an item property `Tone` or `Indent` does the same.
