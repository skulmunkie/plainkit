`pk-label-sheet` lays out labels and cards in fixed physical cells (`cell-width`, `cell-height` in mm, cm, in or pt, `columns`, `gap`, `offset-x`, `offset-y`, `outline` for cut guides); each child is one cell and is never split across a printed page. Use it inside `pk-print-page`. Blazor: `PkLabelSheet`.
