A Guides page, "Responsive design and breakpoints", explains the three named breakpoints (`phone` 640, `tablet` 1024, `wide` 1280), what the elements already do at each, how to respond in your own CSS and scripts (`js/breakpoints.js`), and why other widths need a rebuild. The `plainkit-sdk` and `plainkit-blazor` agent skills carry the same rule, and the generated theming reference lists the breakpoints with what changes at each, taken from the shipped data.
