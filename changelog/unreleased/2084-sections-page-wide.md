`pk-sections-page` shows every section together above `config.tabsFrom` (`phone` or `tablet`, the vocabulary of `pk-detail-layout`), with a section marked `side: true` in the sidebar column, and keeps the tab strip below it; `config.sticky` and `config.stickyFrom` pin the title bar. Without them the page is unchanged. Blazor: `TabsFrom`, `Sticky`, `StickyFrom`.
