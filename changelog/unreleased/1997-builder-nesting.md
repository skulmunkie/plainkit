The layout builder accepts nested components at any depth. A palette element, or an existing element dragged by its new Move chip, drops into the innermost container under the pointer, in the slot it is over (a card's footer, say), at the position an insertion line shows; Alt drops one container out, and a drop the page would refuse is marked and explained. Properties gains a Move into list (the page, or any container and slot) as the keyboard and touch way to do the same.
