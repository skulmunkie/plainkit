`pk-container` is a new element: a max-width, padded content region (`size` on the `--content-*` tokens, `padding` on the space scale, `align`, `fill`) that can scroll on its own (`scroll="y"`/`"both"` with `label`), for a doc-like page, a gallery stage or a code view that used to be a hand-styled `div`.
