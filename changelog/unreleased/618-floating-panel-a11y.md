`pk-dock`'s floating panels get a keyboard and menu path: a Panel menu's Float item detaches a docked panel, a floating panel's own menu offers docking it back into the tree, its frame moves and resizes with arrow keys (Alt+Arrow resizes, Shift steps bigger) once focused, and focusing anything inside a floating panel brings it to the front. A live region announces each of these.
