The Gallery's page frame is a pk-app-shell: the contents nav is its nav column, the preview bar and the full-page bar are its header, and the view is the one scroller with the Details drawer docked over its end edge. Under the site app, which owns the nav and the header, the same parts sit in a plain stack. The bars are about a pixel shorter on a wide screen and ten pixels shorter on a phone.
