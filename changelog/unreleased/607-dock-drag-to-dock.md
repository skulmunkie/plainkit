`pk-dock` panels can be dragged by their header or tab onto another group: drop on its center to add the panel as a tab there, or on an edge (left, right, top, bottom) to split and dock it there. A drop-zone highlight shows the target while dragging. This is the pointer path for the moveTab/dockPanel operations the keyboard/menu Move control already offered; that control keeps working unchanged.
