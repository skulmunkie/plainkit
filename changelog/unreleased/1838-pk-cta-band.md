`pk-cta-band`: a call-to-action surface with your own heading, a line of text and one or two actions (`tone` neutral or brand, `align` start or center, `bleed` for edge to edge). Your markup is never changed, the actions are full width on a phone, and the heading, text and links show as plain blocks before the element upgrades or with JavaScript off.
